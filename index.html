How a forecast is kept

  1. The question says what will happen, by when, and the public source that will settle it.
  2. The commitment. The forecast is a sealed record, and its seal is anchored before the outcome, so anyone can check that it was made first and never changed.
  3. The score. When the question resolves, the outcome and its source are recorded, and the forecast is scored.
  4. The calibration plot shows every resolved forecast, the wrong ones too.
